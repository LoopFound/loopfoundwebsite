

## Loop Found — Web Dev Agency Landing Page

A modern, bold, single-page landing site with smooth scroll sections and a dark navy + gold/amber color scheme matching your brand.

### Design & UX
- **Color palette**: Dark navy (#0a0e27) background, gold/amber (#d4a017) accents, white text
- **Typography**: Bold serif-style headings, clean sans-serif body text
- **Animations**: Fade-in on scroll for sections, subtle hover effects on cards and buttons
- **Fully responsive** for mobile, tablet, and desktop
- **Smooth scrolling** navigation bar at the top
